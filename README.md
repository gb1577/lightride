# LightRide

**Rhythm flying game in your browser: ride the beat through 8 digital realms.**

▶ **[Play in your browser](https://gb1577.github.io/lightride/)**

Pilot your light-rider through 8 digital realms, from Halo City to the top-down Disc Array and Core Stacks to the mirror plains of Digi-Planes, where every ring lands on the beat of the soundtrack. Thread the rings, chain combos and hit dead centre for PERFECT.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse / WASD | Steer (touch: drag anywhere) |
| Space (hold) | Overdrive: double points, burns energy |
| C | Switch camera |
| P / M | Pause / mute |
